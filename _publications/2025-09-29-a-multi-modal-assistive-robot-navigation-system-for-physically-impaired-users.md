Mobility limitations significantly affect the autonomy of individuals with physical disabilities, highlighting the need for advanced assistive technologies. Mobility assistance is particularly crucial for people with conditions such as spinal cord injuries (SCI), heart disease, or those recovering from accidents. This paper introduces a multi-modal mobile robot motion control system that integrates various control methods, including face orientation gestures, voice command detection via the AI-powered JavaScript API, and wireless inputs, to enhance accessibility and ease of use. The system lets users intuitively navigate the robot, minimizing dependence on conventional input devices. Additionally, the system features a remote control web interface that enables caregivers or other users to monitor and operate the robot when necessary. By incorporating intelligent motion control techniques, this research aims to enhance mobility, independence, and overall quality of life for individuals with physical impairments.
