I am a recent Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) graduate from the Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST), Dhaka. I currently work as a Junior Software Engineer in the Research & Development team at TechnoNext Software Limited, and I am a Researcher at the SwAPP Lab, Iowa State University, under the supervision of Prof. Ali Jannesari. My work sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence and human-centered computing, and I enjoy building systems where large language models and intelligent agents solve practical, real-world problems.

My research focuses on **LLM-based agentic systems**, **efficient deep learning and model compression**, **computer vision**, and **human-computer interaction (HCI)**. At the SwAPP Lab, I have worked on structured pruning and post-training quantization of CNN and ViT architectures for LLM-guided model compression, cutting memory and parameter counts while keeping accuracy loss minimal. My other work spans agent-based simulation of user behavior to evaluate website navigability (my undergraduate thesis), a multi-modal assistive robot navigation system, and usability-driven interface design such as a modified keyboard layout for one-handed users. Across these projects, my interest is in making AI systems both more efficient and more usable.

I am currently seeking PhD positions in Computer Science starting Fall 2027. My research interests include large language model agents, efficient and adaptive model optimization, natural language processing, and human-centered AI. I am especially keen to join a group where I can combine rigorous machine learning research with a focus on real-world usability and impact. Feel free to reach out if my interests align with your work.
