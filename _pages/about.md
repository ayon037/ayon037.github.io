I am a recent Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) graduate from Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST), currently working as a Software Engineer. My academic and professional work sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence and human-centered computing, and I enjoy building systems where large language models and intelligent agents solve practical, real-world problems.

My research focuses on **LLM-based agentic systems**, **efficient deep learning**, and **human-computer interaction (HCI)**. I have worked on profiling-guided agents that automate model compression through pruning and quantization, agent-based simulation of user behavior to evaluate website navigability, and usability-driven interface design such as a modified keyboard layout for one-handed users. Across these projects, my interest is in making AI systems both more efficient and more usable.

I am currently seeking PhD positions in Computer Science starting Fall 2027. My research interests include large language model agents, efficient and adaptive model optimization, and human-centered AI. I am especially keen to join a group where I can combine rigorous machine learning research with a focus on real-world usability and impact. Feel free to reach out if my interests align with your work.
